Snapshot

I'm an experienced defence assurance and supply chain professional moving into civilian supply chain assurance and operational controls roles, with third-party risk and GRC as adjacent routes. Eighteen years operating inside mandated assurance frameworks, high-consequence logistics and transport environments, and internal control cycles. The job has always been the same question in different uniforms: does the record match the reality, and how would you know if it stopped? I design controls, investigate discrepancies, improve compliance, and give decision-makers confidence that records reflect reality.

As Tier 1 signatory on the supersession certificate for my account, that is, the person formally accountable for certifying the accuracy of stock and inventory records at handover, I sit inside a four-tier assurance structure that maps directly onto the Three Lines Model: my own first-line ownership of the account, second-line oversight from the Logistics Officer, third-line independent audit, and fourth-line assurance from Navy Command Headquarters (NCHQ). I recognised the mapping only once I began studying the model formally, years after I first signed for the account.
