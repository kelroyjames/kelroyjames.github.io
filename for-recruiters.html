Snapshot

I'm an experienced defence assurance and supply chain professional moving into civilian supply chain risk, third-party assurance and GRC roles. Eighteen years operating inside mandated assurance frameworks, high-consequence transport regimes and internal control cycles: the job has always been the same question in different uniforms: does the record match the reality, and how would you know if it stopped? I design controls, investigate discrepancies, improve compliance, and give decision-makers confidence that records reflect reality.
