All frameworks & reference material

Frameworks informed by real operational problems and adapted into public, civilian-readable tools. The three most complete are Third-Party Risk, End to End, An Internal Audit, End to End and One Register, Every Supplier. All ten are here, and each links to the case study or framework that shows it in use. Each is a public reference framework that draws on published sources and my experience, and none reproduces an internal system, policy or procedure. For a curated view organised by theme, see Supply Chain Assurance and Third-Party Risk & Supplier Assurance.
