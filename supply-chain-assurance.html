Supply Chain Assurance

Eighteen years in Defence logistics have meant one recurring task: checking whether the record matches the physical reality, then building the control that closes the gap when it doesn't. That work spans financial control, asset accountability, compliance recovery and audit remediation across defence, maritime and aviation environments, evidenced below through case studies, working frameworks and an MIT MicroMasters in Supply Chain Management.
