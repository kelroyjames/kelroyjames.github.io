Background

Evidence before credentials

I started as a meter reader for the national electricity utility in St Vincent and spent twelve years there, finishing in planning and GIS. I led the GPS geo-tagging of the island's grid infrastructure, and that dataset went on to underpin an enterprise mapping system that won an Esri Special Achievement in GIS award.

I joined the Royal Navy and have spent eighteen years in Defence logistics: supply accounts, stocktaking, supersession and handover, fleet logistics inspection, transport safety and remedial action. It was presented to me as logistics. It took a postgraduate degree and a set of audit qualifications before I recognised it as governance, risk and compliance, which is a large part of why I now write about the translation problem.

From April 2022 I served with Wildcat Maritime Force, where I consolidated dispersed ground support equipment into a single accountable stock holding site and closed fourteen major loss investigations along the way. The same posting took me to Gibraltar, to turn round a rejected consignment against a fixed flight deadline for a weapons trial, and to Norway, to move seven containers of equipment for a joint exercise and recover a mid-exercise equipment shortfall with three hours to spare. Leading the seven-person team through those deployments, I ran a Mission Model Canvas programme to build their strategic awareness of innovation — five of the seven have since been promoted — and the tour was recognised with a Royal Navy Inclusivity Award for Innovation.

From there I moved to a Type 45 destroyer, where I took over a supply chain account that had been assessed as unsatisfactory. I designed the first-line controls that brought it to full compliance, identified and corrected £2.4M in misallocated expenditure, and co-developed the STOROB inventory interrogation application in Palantir Foundry with BAE Systems and DE&S. I'm currently ranked in the top three of seventy-nine peer supply chain senior rates across Surface Flotilla HQ.

I also founded "Empowerment Day", a development programme for junior rates adopted as a standing programme by Engineering Support Group 1.
