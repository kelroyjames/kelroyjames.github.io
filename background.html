Background · Study · Qualifications

Background

From a meter reader in St Vincent to eighteen years in Defence logistics, and the study, qualifications and programmes that bridge to civilian assurance work. A short version is on the homepage; this is the full story.

Past

I started as a meter reader for the national electricity utility in St Vincent, and spent twelve years working up to leading the GPS geo-tagging project that underpinned an Esri Special Achievement in GIS award. I joined the Royal Navy and spent eighteen years in Defence logistics: supply accounts, stocktaking, fleet inspection, transport safety. It took a postgraduate degree and a set of audit qualifications to recognise it as governance, risk and compliance under a different name. At Wildcat Maritime Force, I consolidated scattered equipment into one accountable site, closed fourteen loss investigations, and led a seven-person team through a live weapons trial and an overseas exercise, work recognised with a Royal Navy Inclusivity Award for Innovation.

Present

On a Type 45 destroyer, I took over a supply chain account rated unsatisfactory, identified £2.4M in misallocated expenditure and led the analysis supporting its correction, and reduced financial liability by 80% through asset verification and documentation remediation. I co-developed the STOROB inventory application in Palantir Foundry with BAE Systems and DE&S, founded "Empowerment Day" for junior rates, and I'm currently ranked in the top three of seventy-nine peer senior rates across Surface Flotilla HQ, assessed against the whole peer group, not just my own trade.

Future

I'm building towards a civilian role in supply chain assurance, third-party risk, GRC or operational controls, with internal audit as an adjacent route, at a defence prime, professional services firm or bank, applying the same discipline, closing the gap between what the records say and what's physically true, to supplier ecosystems instead of naval stores accounts. The MSc, the ISO lead auditor qualifications and the case studies on this site are the bridge, and the evidence that it holds.
